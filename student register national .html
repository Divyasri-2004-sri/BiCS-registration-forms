Purpose of BiCS 2026

The objectives of the Blue Intelligence Climate Summit (BiCS) 2026 are centred on the synergy between marine preservation and technological innovation.

  • Encourage innovation in marine technology and fisheries
  • Strengthen maritime safety and satellite-based solutions
  • Facilitate policy dialogue and industry collaboration
  • Support coastal community development and livelihoods
Takeaways for Participants
  • Campus to Coast: Activating young minds and researchers to transition theoretical marine science and data prototypes into actionable real-world applications.
  • Industrial Transition (Shipping & Industrialists): Deploying AI-driven logistics and route optimisation tools to rapidly lower emissions and support cleaner maritime supply chains.
  • Resilient Economies (Local Govt & Eco-Tourism): Empowering public officials and local hospitality businesses with data to build policies that safeguard fragile coastal ecosystems while driving sustainable local tourism.
Awards
  • Top 1 poster of one Student will receive a Cash Award 2026
  • Top 1 paper presentation of one Researcher will receive a Cash Award 2026
  • Surprise "Cash Awards" or "Cash Vouchers" from various industries
  • Multiple technical tracks — Top 3 awarded as Title Winners
  • Top research papers fast-tracked into prestigious journals like Marine Policy and Applied Ocean Research — Top 3 awarded as Title Winners
Topics for Students
  • The Smart Port Challenge — Shipping Logistics
  • The co-Pulse Track — Eco-Tourism App
  • The Civic Blueprint — Coastal Policy
