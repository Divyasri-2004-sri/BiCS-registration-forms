About BiCS 2026

The Blue Intelligence Climate Summit 2026 is an action-oriented, cross-sector event designed to pioneer data-driven solutions for ocean climate action. By bridging the gap between advanced technology and marine conservation, the summit serves as a collaborative incubator where young talent, academic rigor, and industrial operations converge to build a sustainable blue economy.

Key Highlights
  • Hackathon Pool: Multiple technical tracks challenging participants to solve real-world industrial and civic problems.
  • Scopus-Indexed Publications: Top research papers fast-tracked into prestigious journals like Marine Policy and Applied Ocean Research.
  • Surprise Cash Awards: For best 1st & 2nd projects of Students and Research Categories.
  • High-Value Sponsorships: Corporate-backed tiers (Blue Whale, Shark, Dolphin) driving direct industry alignment and recruitment pipelines.
Purpose

The objectives of the Blue Intelligence Climate Summit (BiCS) 2026 are centered on the synergy between marine preservation and technological innovation:

  • Encourage innovation in marine technology and fisheries
  • Strengthen maritime safety and satellite-based solutions
  • Facilitate policy dialogue and industry collaboration
  • Support coastal community development and livelihoods
