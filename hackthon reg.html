About BiCS 2026:

The Blue Intelligence Climate Summit 2026 aims to bring together policymakers, researchers, industry leaders, and coastal community stakeholders to promote sustainable ocean resource management aligned with SDG 14 (Life Below Water).

The Blue Intelligence Climate Summit (BICS) 2026 is a premier 2-day event uniting advanced technology and marine conservation. BICS 2026 serves as an action-oriented incubator where youth innovation, academic rigor, and industrial operations converge to co-create scalable AI solutions, decarbonize global shipping, and secure sustainable blue economies.

The Blue Intelligence Climate Summit 2026 is an action-oriented, cross-sector event designed to pioneer data-driven solutions for ocean climate action. By bridging the gap between advanced technology and marine conservation, the summit serves as a collaborative incubator where young talent, academic rigor, and industrial operations converge to build a sustainable blue economy. The details of the BiCS 2026 are here:

  • Dates: 2nd & 3rd October 2026
  • Location: India
  • Core Focus: Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, Marine Science, and Maritime Logistics.
  • Location Strategy: Targeted at coastal innovation hubs to maximize local governance and maritime industry participation.
  • Theme: Blue Intelligence For A Sustainable Oceans
  • Hackathon Pool: Multiple technical tracks challenging students to solve real-world industrial and civic problems.
